The small print

Good to know

Can a package be customised?
Yes. Every package here is a starting point — tell us the number of guests, the nights and anything else the evening needs, and we build the quote around that.
How do we pay?
Nothing is paid online. Once the quote is agreed, our team sends the invoice and the payment details, by bank transfer for companies.
Do you issue invoices for companies?
Yes. Give us your company details and ICE number with the request and the invoice is issued in the company's name.
Can we book for several nights?
Yes. Boxes and spaces can be held for more than one night, and the 10-Day Corporate Pass covers every night of the festival.
