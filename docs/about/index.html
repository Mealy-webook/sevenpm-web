Who we are

Founded in 2018, SEVENPM was born from the desire to take cultural events in Morocco to new heights.

Since then, SEVENPM has established itself as an essential player, orchestrating with passion and dedication iconic festivals such as Jazzablanca, Tanjazz, Village Casa Anfa and Casa Anfa Latina.

  • 01

    Mission

    Offer an exceptional experience to our festivalgoers and our whole community by guaranteeing access to culture and events.

  • 02

    Vision

    Build a model of cultural enterprise that brings the world's best artists to Morocco and puts young Moroccan talent in the spotlight.

  • 03

    Values

    Encouraging diversity and promoting social inclusion in partnership with committed businesses and our communities to make culture accessible to all.
